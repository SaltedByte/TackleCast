# TackleCast v2.3.VI — Greetings from Vice City

*Draft release copy. “v2.3.VI” is edition branding; the application/package version is 2.3.0.*

Sunset colors. Familiar fish. Your console, front and center.

TackleCast VI brings an original coastal look and a configurable performance pill to the same lightweight capture-card viewer. No characters, no copied game artwork, and no effects applied to your captured picture.

## Welcome to Vice City

- New Vice theme: midnight-purple panels, warm text, peach/pink accents, and an original palm-lined waterfront header.
- Remade fish-and-broadcast icon with a sunset palette.
- Cleaner settings presentation while retaining Video, Picture, Audio, and Display.
- The scenery stays in settings. Close the menu and the game takes over.

## Wanted: your stats, your rules

- Choose which statistics appear: resolution, delivered FPS, target/nominal FPS, pixel format, scaling filter, color space, and color range.
- Drag a grip to reorder entries, or use Up/Down buttons. Checkbox choices move with their rows.
- Put the pill in any corner, hide it entirely, or turn every statistic off without losing the other settings.
- Compact, two-values-per-line presentation with a quieter secondary row.
- Fractional target frame rates retain their exact ratio; scaling labels retain actual renderer fallback information.

## Lose the tail, not the signal

- Capture and reconnect messages remain visible even when performance statistics are disabled.
- Capture failures do not leave stale delivered-FPS readings on screen.

## Back to familiar streets

- Switch to Classic in Display to restore the familiar navy/coral palette.
- Theme and overlay choices preview in the menu and persist on close without requiring a capture restart.
- Existing overlay preferences migrate to the new customizable list.

## Same getaway driver

This edition does not replace the video, decoding, scaling, or audio pipelines. It adds no recording and makes no new capture-latency or maximum-performance claims. Existing device, operating-system, and hardware requirements still apply.

TackleCast remains free and open source. This is an unofficial, independently created themed edition; it is not affiliated with or endorsed by Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive.
